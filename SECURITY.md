# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues, discussions or pull requests.**

Send a detailed report to **[security@save5hours.ai](mailto:security@save5hours.ai)** and include:

- The affected repository, service or endpoint
- Steps to reproduce, including any proof of concept
- The potential impact and, if known, affected versions
- Any logs, screenshots or supporting material

## What to expect

| Stage | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Acknowledgement of your report | Within 3 business days |
| Initial assessment and severity | Within 5 business days |
| Status updates until resolution | At least every 10 business days |

We will let you know when the issue is resolved and, if you would like, credit you in the
disclosure.

## Scope

This policy applies to all repositories under the
[Save5Hours](https://github.com/Save5Hours) organization and to the services we operate on
behalf of clients.

Please avoid privacy violations, data destruction, service degradation and any access to
client data while researching. Only test against assets you are authorised to test.

## Client environments

Client deployments run in isolated environments. If a report affects a specific client
system, say so explicitly in your email so we can involve the right people under the
appropriate confidentiality terms.
