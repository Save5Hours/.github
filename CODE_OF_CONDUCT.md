# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We expect professional, respectful and constructive collaboration across all Save5Hours
repositories, issues, pull requests and discussions.

**Expected:**

- Assume good intent and give direct, specific, actionable feedback
- Keep discussions technical, factual and focused on the problem
- Respect confidentiality around client systems, data and environments

**Not acceptable:**

- Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks in any form
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Deliberate disruption of discussions, reviews or project work
- Sharing client data, credentials or environment details in public channels

## Scope

This applies to every space managed by the organization, and to anyone representing
Save5Hours in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to
**[hello@save5hours.ai](mailto:hello@save5hours.ai)**. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone who violates this policy.

Security issues are handled separately and must not be disclosed publicly — see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
